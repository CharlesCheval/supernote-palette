## What's new

- **Dashes, fills and hatching land in the right place on pages made for another device** (an A5X/A6X note opened on a Manta, or the other way round). Like Snap, Palette now converts pen coordinates with the screen size, then scales them to the page.

Install: copy `Palette.snplg` to `MyStyle`, then **Settings → Apps → Plugins → Add plugin**. Your settings are kept.
