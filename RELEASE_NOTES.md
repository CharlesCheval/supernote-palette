## What's new

- **Landscape:** dashes, fills and hatching on shapes (for example circles made with Snap) were drawn at 3/4 of their place and size. They now land on the shape.
- **Pages made for another device** (an A5X/A6X note opened on a Manta): dashes, fills and hatching on strokes now land where the strokes are.

Install: copy `Palette.snplg` to `MyStyle`, then **Settings → Apps → Plugins → Add plugin**. Your settings are kept.
